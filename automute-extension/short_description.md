# AutoMute - Smart Ad Detection and Auto-Mute

Automatically mute video ads while preserving sports and game audio using AI-powered content detection.

## Short Description (132 characters max)

## Detailed Description

AutoMute uses advanced AI to distinguish between video advertisements and sports content, automatically muting ads while keeping sports audio unmuted.

**Key Features:**
• 🤖 AI-powered content detection using GPT-4 Vision
• 🎥 Automatically mutes YouTube and streaming video ads
• 🏈 Preserves audio for sports content (NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, Soccer, etc.)
• 📺 Works with major streaming platforms (YouTube, ESPN, Fox Sports, etc.)
• 🔇 Smart detection - only mutes VIDEO ads, not static webpage banners
• ⚡ Real-time monitoring with 2.5-second intervals
• 🎛️ Manual override controls available
• 📊 Classification history and confidence scoring
• 🔒 Privacy-focused - screenshots deleted immediately after analysis

**How It Works:**
1. Select any tab you want to monitor from the extension popup
2. Click "Start AutoMuting" 
3. The extension captures screenshots every 2.5 seconds
4. AI analyzes the content and classifies it as "ad", "sports", or "other"
5. Video ads are automatically muted, sports content stays unmuted
6. Switch tabs freely - monitoring continues in the background

**Perfect For:**
• Sports fans who want to skip ads but keep game audio
• YouTube viewers tired of video ad interruptions
• Streaming enthusiasts watching live sports
• Anyone who wants smarter ad blocking with audio control

**Supported Content:**
• YouTube videos and live streams

**Privacy & Security:**

• No personal data collection or tracking
