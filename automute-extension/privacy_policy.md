# AutoMute Privacy Policy

## Data Collection and Use

AutoMute is designed with privacy in mind. Here's what data we collect and how we use it:

### Screenshots
- **What**: Screenshots of web pages you choose to monitor
- **When**: Only when you explicitly start monitoring a tab
- **How**: Screenshots are captured locally and sent to our secure API for AI analysis
- **Storage**: Screenshots are immediately deleted after analysis and are never stored permanently
- **Purpose**: To identify ads and sports content for automatic audio control

### API Communication
- **What**: Encrypted HTTPS communication with our classification service
- **Data**: Only the screenshot image and timestamp
- **Security**: All communications use SSL/TLS encryption
- **Retention**: No data is stored on our servers after processing

### Local Storage
- **What**: Extension settings and classification history (last 50 results)
- **Where**: Stored locally in your browser only
- **Access**: Only accessible by the AutoMute extension
- **Removal**: Deleted when you uninstall the extension

## What We Don't Collect
- Personal information or browsing history
- Audio or video content from your tabs
- Passwords, form data, or sensitive information
- Data from tabs you're not monitoring
- Analytics or tracking data

## Third-Party Services
- **OpenAI**: We use OpenAI's API for image classification. Screenshots are sent securely and are not stored by OpenAI.
- **Google Cloud**: Our API is hosted on Google Cloud Run with enterprise-grade security.

## Your Rights
- You can stop monitoring at any time
- You can clear local data by uninstalling the extension
- You have full control over which tabs are monitored
